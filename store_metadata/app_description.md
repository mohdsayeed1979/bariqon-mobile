# App Store Description

Drafted from the app's own in-app "About" copy (`aboutTagline`/`aboutDescription`
in `lib/l10n/app_en.arb`) for a consistent brand voice — that in-app copy is
itself flagged as **"placeholder pending final marketing copy,"** so review
and adjust this alongside it before submission; don't treat either as final
without a human sign-off from whoever owns Bariqon's marketing voice.

Everything below describes only what the app actually does today — no
checkout/payment language, since there isn't one (the app is inquiry/quote
based, not e-commerce), and no feature is mentioned that isn't implemented
(e.g. push notifications aren't claimed — the Settings toggles for them are
UI-only today, not wired to real delivery).

## Description (4000 characters max)

```
Bariqon Trading is a Bahrain-based company specializing in luxury gift
boxes, hospitality supplies, and custom packaging for businesses and
individuals across the region. The Bariqon app brings that catalog to your
phone — browse, search, and send an inquiry directly to our team.

CATALOG
• Browse products by category, with real photos and descriptions
• Search the full catalog by name
• View detailed specifications and features for each product

INQUIRIES, NOT CHECKOUT
Bariqon works on a request-a-quote basis — add the products you're
interested in to your inquiry list, fill in your contact details, and
submit. Our team follows up directly; there's no in-app payment.

ACCOUNTS
• Create an account to save your profile for faster inquiries, or browse
  as a guest
• Manage your profile and contact details at any time

BUILT FOR HOW YOU WORK
• Full English and Arabic support, including right-to-left layout
• Light, dark, and system-matched appearance
• Designed for iPhone and iPad

Bariqon Trading — premium presentation and dependable service on every
order.
```
(1,180 characters — well under the 4000 limit; room to expand once real
marketing copy replaces the placeholder About text)
